About This Site

Made by hand with HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript. No frameworks, no tracking.

The colors come from 512 Pixels. The idea of a website that is also a desktop comes from the Beautiful Mind forum.

Set in EB Garamond and ChicagoFLF, a public-domain revival of Susan Kare’s Chicago. The icons are Apple’s own, from Mac OS 8. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
